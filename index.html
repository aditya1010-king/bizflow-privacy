BizFlow Privacy Policy

Simple digital cash book for small businesses

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Your data stays on your device

BizFlow is a local, offline cash book. Your transactions are stored on your device, and BizFlow itself never sends them anywhere — the app does not even request internet access.

There is one exception, described in full below: Android's own backup system. If you have backup switched on in your device settings, Android may copy BizFlow's data to your personal Google account. That is done by Android, not by BizFlow.

What we collect

BizFlow does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal or financial information. All transactions, notes, amounts, and payment details you enter are stored in a local database on your device.

BizFlow declares no internet permission in its Android manifest, so the app is technically incapable of sending your cash book data to us or to anyone else. We operate no servers and have no account system — there is nothing to sign up for and no profile associated with you.

Microphone access

BizFlow's Voice Entry feature uses your device's built-in speech recognition to convert what you say into text, so you can fill in an entry faster than typing it.

BizFlow does not record, store, or transmit audio. When you tap Voice Entry, the audio is handed to the Android speech recognition service on your device, which returns text. Depending on your device and settings, that service may process speech on-device or on Google's servers; that processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and your device settings, not by BizFlow. The microphone is only active while the Voice Entry dialog is open, and you can decline the permission and use BizFlow normally by typing instead.

Storage access

BizFlow requests storage access only to save and share the PDF cash-book reports that you generate. On Android 9 and older this needs the storage permission; on newer versions of Android no permission is required.

These PDF files are created only when you ask for them, are written to your device's Documents folder, and remain there until you delete or share them. BizFlow never uploads them.

Android backup

BizFlow allows Android's standard backup system to include its data, so that your cash book can be restored if you lose your phone or move to a new one. When backup is enabled on your device, Android may copy BizFlow's local database and settings to your own Google account.

This transfer is performed by the Android operating system, not by BizFlow, and the resulting backup is stored in your personal Google account, not ours. We have no access to it. You can turn it off on your device under Settings ▸ Google ▸ Backup.

Third-party sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party, ad network, or analytics service. BizFlow contains no advertising, no trackers, no analytics SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs.

The only ways BizFlow data can leave your device are the two you control directly: Android's built-in backup, described above, and any PDF report you choose to share yourself.

Children's privacy

BizFlow is a business bookkeeping tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, and in fact collect no personal information from anyone.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data never reaches us, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Your records live in the app's local database for as long as you keep them. You can delete individual transactions inside the app, and you can remove all BizFlow data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, under Settings ▸ Apps ▸ BizFlow ▸ Storage. If you have Android backup enabled, remove the backup from your Google account settings as well.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top. Material changes will also be reflected in the Privacy Policy screen inside the app.
